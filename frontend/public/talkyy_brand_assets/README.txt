Talkyy brand board extraction
==============================

These are raster crops extracted from the supplied Talkyy Premium Brand Identity Board.
They are not original vector/source logo files. Fine details and typography remain limited
by the resolution and rendering of the supplied board.

Included:
- Primary logo with tagline
- Light and dark app icon presentations
- Primary logo, symbol-only, wordmark-only, dark-background, and monochrome variants
- Brand color palette and usage examples
- Signage and embossed mockups

Brand colors visually shown on the board:
- Cobalt: #2858E8
- Deep Blue: #173BB5
- Mint: #B9D9C7
- Soft Blue: #E8EEFF
- Warm Paper: #F7F5EF
- Ink: #191B20

For production use, recreate/export the chosen mark as SVG or a high-resolution transparent PNG
from a vector design tool. The mockups and color palette are presentation crops, not standalone
editable brand assets.
